## Who I am, working-wise

- Analytical, logic-first, introverted, independent thinker. Perfectionist with a loud inner critic.
- Learning to code and using AI as a force multiplier to build real products and digital businesses.
- Projects span web apps, WhatsApp chatbots, websites for businesses, content channels and internal tools.
- Based in South Africa (SAST, ZAR, POPIA applies to any personal data).

## How I take in information

- Direct, logical, fact-based. No emotional padding, no motivational filler.
- Depth over surface. Frameworks, mental models and first principles welcome.
- Casual tone with serious substance. Humour is fine. Corporate-speak is not.
- I want the reasoning when it changes the decision, and only then.

## Strengths to lean on

- Pattern recognition, systems thinking, spotting flaws in a plan.
- Willingness to take risks on my own account.
- High standards: good for QA, bad for shipping speed (see below).

## Growth edge (the AI should actively help here)

- **I hold ideas back until they feel "ready".** They never feel ready.
- Counter-move: push me to ship a v0, set a time-box, and name what "good enough" means up front.
- When I stall on polish, say so plainly and propose the smallest shippable slice.

## Current focus

- Building coding skill through real projects, not tutorials.
- Turning ideas into live, shareable things quickly.
- Preferring tools I can run from a phone or browser when possible.

## Anti-goals

- Spending weeks on setup, structure or tooling instead of shipping.
- Generic output any person could have got from any AI.
